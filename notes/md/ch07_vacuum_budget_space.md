### Chapter 7: Vacuum, Sectors, Energy, Budget

*Assumption:* From this chapter onward, we strictly require the lattice size $L = 2h$ to be an even integer. This guarantees the existence of a perfectly half-filled Fermi sea in the asymmetric dual band $\Lambda^* = \{-h+1, \dots, h\}$. The mathematical formulation of the budget bounds follows [Appendix A03](../appendices/a03_energy_budgets_and_filtered_maps.md).

**Intuition for the "Energy Budget":**
In continuum quantum field theory, the Dirac sea has infinite depth. Taking the limit $L \to \infty$ naively results in unbounded operators and divergences. To formalize this rigorously without resorting to topology, we use a truncation scheme called the **Energy Budget**, restricting our operators and states to a finite-dimensional subspace governed by excitation energy ($K$) and net charge deviation ($N_{\max}$).

---

#### 7.1 Definitions

**Definition 7.1 (Dirac Vacuum).**
The vacuum state subset $S_\Omega \subseteq \Lambda^*$ corresponds to the exactly $h$ occupied modes $k \le 0$:

$$
S_\Omega := \{k \in \Lambda^* \mid k \le 0\} \tag{7.1}
$$

The vacuum state vector is defined on the Fock basis as $|\Omega\rangle := \delta_{S_\Omega}$.

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
To formalize the Dirac vacuum, we can define `S_Omega` as a `Finset Int` (or an appropriate set over `Lambda_star` depending on how the dual band is modeled, e.g., a `Finset` of half-integers or shifted integers). Since the physical modes $k$ are defined in a finite band $\{-h+1, \dots, h\}$, we can express $S_\Omega$ as `{k \in \Lambda^* | k \le 0}`.
The state vector $|\Omega\rangle$ can be defined using `Finsupp` or a specialized `Basis` construction on the Fock space, mapping `S_Omega` to 1 and all other configurations to 0. 
**Auxiliary Lemmas:** 
- A lemma proving that `S_Omega` has cardinality `h`.
- A lemma showing `S_Omega` is a valid subset of $\Lambda^*$.

**Definition 7.2 (Integer Charge and Energy).**
To avoid natural subtraction anomalies, we define all fundamental combinatorial quantities strictly in $\mathbb{Z}$:

$$
N(S) := (\#S : \mathbb{Z}) - h \tag{7.2}
$$
$$
P(S) := \sum_{k \in S} k - \sum_{k \le 0} k \tag{7.3}
$$

The ground-energy integer $t(N)$ is defined as the unique integer satisfying $2 t(N) = N(N+1)$. The true excitation energy is precisely:

$$
e(S) := P(S) - t(N(S)) \tag{7.4}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
We can define $N(S)$ and $P(S)$ as computable functions returning `Int`. 
- `N (S : Finset Int) : Int := S.card - h`
- `P (S : Finset Int) : Int := (∑ k in S, k) - (∑ k in S_Omega, k)`
The ground-energy integer $t(N)$ can be computed as `(N * (N + 1)) / 2`, and its uniqueness as the solution to `2 * t = N * (N + 1)` can be formalized as an `iff` theorem. 
The excitation energy $e(S) := P(S) - t(N(S))$ directly follows.
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- `t_N_is_integer`: Proof that `N * (N + 1)` is always even, meaning `t(N)` is a well-defined integer without division anomalies.
- `P_Omega_zero`: Proof that `P(S_Omega) = 0`.
- `e_Omega_zero`: Proof that `e(S_Omega) = 0`.

**Definition 7.3 (Budget Subspaces).**
We explicitly distinguish three orthogonal coordinate spans of the occupation basis:
1. **Fixed-Energy Space:** $H(N,E) := \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{C}} \{ \delta_S \mid N(S) = N \land e(S) = E \}$.
2. **Fixed-Charge Budget:** $B(N,K) := \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{C}} \{ \delta_S \mid N(S) = N \land e(S) \le K \}$.
3. **Charge-Box Budget:** $\mathcal{B}_{K,N_{\max}} := \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{C}} \{ \delta_S \mid e(S) \le K \land |N(S)| \le N_{\max} \}$.

A coordinate projection $P_K$ deletes coefficients outside the subset, acting perfectly as an exact orthogonal projection without analytic completions.

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
Budget subspaces can be formalized as `Submodule ℂ FockSpace`, generated by the respective configurations.
- `H (N E : Int) : Submodule ℂ FockSpace := Submodule.span ℂ {δ_S | N(S) = N ∧ e(S) = E}`
- `B (N K : Int) : Submodule ℂ FockSpace := Submodule.span ℂ {δ_S | N(S) = N ∧ e(S) ≤ K}`
- `B_box (K N_max : Int) : Submodule ℂ FockSpace := Submodule.span ℂ {δ_S | e(S) ≤ K ∧ |N(S)| ≤ N_max}`
The projection $P_K$ can be formalized as a `LinearMap` that sets coefficients to 0 if $e(S) > K$.
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- Lemmas proving the orthogonality of these spans for differing energy or charge.
- Proof that $P_K$ is idempotent (`P_K ∘ P_K = P_K`) and self-adjoint.

#### 7.2 Properties of the Vacuum, Energy, and the Budget Subspace

**Lemma 7.4 (Rank Formula and Monotonic Displacements).**
For an arbitrary configuration sorted from lowest to highest, $s_1 < s_2 < \dots < s_{h+N}$, the perfect ground reference is $g_i = -h+1+i$ (0-based index). The independent displacement is $d_i = s_i - g_i$. We rigorously establish that $d_i \ge 0$ and the sequence is monotonically non-decreasing.

$$
e(S) = \sum_i d_i \tag{7.5}
$$

This guarantees algebraically that $e(S) \ge 0$, and the sector ground kets ($|N\rangle_0$) are precisely the unique vectors satisfying $e(S) = 0$.

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
We can map a configuration `S` to a sorted sequence using `Finset.sort` (with the natural order on `Int`). 
Let `s` be the `Fin` indexed array of size `h + N`. The perfect ground reference is `g i := -h + 1 + i`. 
The displacement `d i := s i - g i`. 
To prove $d_i \ge 0$, we proceed by induction on $i$ and use the pigeonhole principle on the available discrete slots in the band. 
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- `g_is_minimal`: Proof that any strictly increasing sequence `s` in $\Lambda^*$ satisfies `s i \ge g i`.
- `e_eq_sum_d`: Proof that $e(S) = \sum_i d_i$.
- `e_nonneg`: Proof that $e(S) \ge 0$ for all $S$.
- `e_zero_iff_ground`: Proof that $e(S) = 0 \iff S$ is the standard ground state for its charge sector.

**Lemma 7.5 (Frozen Margins).**
Because the sequence $d_i$ is non-decreasing and non-negative, the sum $e(S) \le K$ tightly binds the configuration:
1. For all $k \le N-K$, the mode is rigidly full.
2. For all $k > N+K$, the mode is rigidly empty.

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
Given $\sum d_i = e(S) \le K$ and $d_i \ge 0$, we have $d_i \le K$ for all $i$. 
We formalize this by showing that if a mode deep in the Fermi sea (e.g., $k \le N-K$) were empty, or a mode high above ($k > N+K$) were occupied, it would require a displacement sum exceeding $K$.
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- `empty_deep_implies_large_e`: If a state is missing a particle at $k \le N-K$, its excitation energy must be $> K$.
- `occupied_high_implies_large_e`: If a state has a particle at $k > N+K$, its excitation energy must be $> K$.

**Lemma 7.6 (Filtered Operator Action).**
An operator $A$ with energy shift $d \ge 0$ maps the budget $B(N,K)$ strictly into the expanded budget $B(N, K+d)$. It does not act as an endomorphism on $B(N,K)$. For negative shifts $d < 0$, we apply strict integer cutoffs to handle annihilation below the ground state. If a compressed endomorphism is required, it must be explicitly defined as $A_K := P_K A$.

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
This is formalized by defining the concept of a bounded energy shift for operators. 
An operator $A$ has energy shift $\le d$ if for all basis states $\delta_S$, $A(\delta_S)$ is supported only on states $S'$ with $e(S') \le e(S) + d$.
The lemma then states that such an operator maps `B(N, K)` into `B(N, K+d)` (or with a charge shift).
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- `map_budget_le`: Proof that $\forall x \in B(N,K), A x \in B(N, K+d)$.
- Definition and well-definedness of the compressed operator $A_K := P_K \circ A \circ P_K$ (or $P_K \circ A$ on the domain).

**Lemma 7.7 (Composition Margin Accounting).**
When composing operators, partial equality must respect target margins. If $A = B$ strictly on a subspace $W$, and an operator $C$ maps subspace $V$ into $W$, then exactly $AC = BC$ on $V$. When determining the required uniform band cutoffs, the budget $K$ must explicitly include the maximum cumulative upward excursion of intermediate words:

$$
2M + K_{\text{excursion}} + N_{\max} \le h \tag{7.6}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
This formalizes that exact operator equality within the budget requires tracking intermediate excursions. 
If $A$ and $B$ are equal on $W$, and $C : V \to W$, we formalize `A ∘ C x = B ∘ C x` for `x \in V`. 
For practical band cutoffs, we relate this to the `Frozen Margins` (Lemma 7.5): to ensure that intermediate states don't "hit" the top or bottom of the band $\Lambda^*$, the maximum intermediate energy $K_{excursion}$ and charge $N_{max}$ must satisfy $2M + K_{excursion} + N_{max} \le h$, meaning the "active window" stays within $[-h+1, h]$.
**Auxiliary Lemmas:**
- `comp_eq_on_submodule`: General linear algebra lemma for equality of composed maps on a restricted domain.
- `no_band_collision`: Proof that under the condition $2M + K_{excursion} + N_{max} \le h$, the required empty/filled margins strictly respect the band edges.
