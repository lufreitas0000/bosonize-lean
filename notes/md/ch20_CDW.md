### Chapter 20: CDW Correlators and the Topological Duality Theorem

We evaluate macroscopic order parameters like **Charge Density Wave (CDW)** and anomalous pairing algebraically. The formalization strictly follows [Appendix A05](../appendices/a05_exponentials_klein_and_vertex_scope.md), [Appendix A08](../appendices/a08_states_and_correlations.md), and [Appendix A09](../appendices/a09_duality_spin_and_umklapp.md).

#### 20.1 Macroscopic Order Parameters on the Lattice

**Definition 20.1 (CDW and Pairing Order Parameters).**
The local macroscopic order parameters on the discrete lattice $\Lambda$ are defined strictly using fundamental fermions:
$$
O_{CDW}(x) := c^\dagger_{(R, x)} c_{(L, x)} \tag{20.1}
$$
$$
O_{SC}(x) := c_{(R, x)} c_{(L, x)} \tag{20.2}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Define structures for `CDWOrderParameter` and `SCOrderParameter` in the discrete lattice algebra.
- Both must be expressed as elements of the fermion algebra generated by $c_{R, x}, c_{L, x}$, keeping track of lattice indices.
- Ensure the grading/parity of these operators is even, meaning they commute with the total fermion number parity.

**Lemma 20.2 (Bosonized Order Parameters).**
Applying the exact Mattis-Mandelstam dictionary (Theorem 14.7), we map these to bosonic vertex operators. We retain the exact Klein operators and prefactors:
$$
O_{CDW}(x) = \frac{1}{L} F_R^\dagger F_L e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{L}(N_R - N_L)x} \exp\left[ W^-_R(x) - W^-_L(x) \right] \exp\left[ W^+_R(x) - W^+_L(x) \right] \tag{20.3}
$$
$$
O_{SC}(x) = \frac{1}{L} F_R F_L e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{L}(N_R + N_L)x} \exp\left[ W^-_R(x) + W^-_L(x) \right] \exp\left[ W^+_R(x) + W^+_L(x) \right] \tag{20.4}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Use the formal bosonization map (`AlgEquiv` between fermionic and bosonic Fock spaces, preserving grading).
- Provide an auxiliary lemma expanding the point-split products $c^\dagger_{(R, x)} c_{(L, x)}$ through the limits of Mattis-Mandelstam.
- Carefully manage the ordering of the zero-mode (Klein) factors $F_R^\dagger, F_L$ and their commutation relations.
- Normal-ordering terms $W^+, W^-$ must be explicitly managed with `BCH` (Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff) expansions.

#### 20.2 Exact Evaluation of the Vertex Correlators

**Definition 20.3 (Logarithmic Kernel).**
We explicitly define the finite algebraic logarithmic kernel:
$$
D_1(x, y) := \sum_{m=1}^{M} \frac{1}{m} \left( 1 - \cos\left( \frac{2\pi m (x-y)}{L} \right) \right) \tag{20.5}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Define $D_1$ as a map from $\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$.
- Prove its positivity and boundedness for finite $M$.
- Prove translation invariance: $D_1(x, y) = D_1(x - y, 0)$.

**Theorem 20.4 (Exact Finite-Lattice Correlators).**
Applying the exact algebraic BCH formula (Lemma 8.8) and evaluating over the functional $\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}}$, the correlators evaluate to exact equalities (not proportionalities), retaining all regularization dependence and zero-mode weights $C_0$:
$$
\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}} \left( O_{CDW}^\dagger(x) O_{CDW}(y) \right) = \frac{C_0}{L^2} e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{L}\Delta N(x-y)} \exp \left[ -2g \cdot D_1(x, y) \right] \tag{20.6}
$$
$$
\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}} \left( O_{SC}^\dagger(x) O_{SC}(y) \right) = \frac{C_0'}{L^2} e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{L}\Sigma N(x-y)} \exp \left[ -\frac{2}{g} \cdot D_1(x, y) \right] \tag{20.7}
$$
*(Note: A finite polynomial exponential calculation is a polynomial in its scalar parameters; it does not generally equal a scalar `Complex.exp` without a separate analytic interpretation.)*

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Evaluate the state functional $\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}}$ on products of exponentials using the exact BCH algebraic simplifications.
- Provide an auxiliary lemma to decouple the Klein zero-modes expectations ($C_0, C_0'$) from the bosonic modes.
- Combine the finite polynomial exponential expressions into the scalar $D_1(x, y)$ form using the definition of $D_1$.

#### 20.3 Topological Duality Isomorphism

An exchange $\varphi \leftrightarrow \theta$ is not by itself an `AlgEquiv` of a fixed finite budget endomorphism algebra. It maps the charge lattice to a dual parity-constrained sublattice.

**Theorem 20.5 (The Exact Duality Map).**
We define the Duality map $\mathcal{D}$ strictly as an equivalence between two explicitly related models and their mapped budgets. It is defined on the fundamental generators, mapping the vacuum, charge sectors, and energy cutoffs explicitly. Under $\mathcal{D}$:
$$
\mathcal{D} \left( H_{\text{Lutt}}(g) \right) = H_{\text{Lutt}}(1/g) \tag{20.8}
$$
The order parameters rigorously map into each other, preserving all phases, adjoints, and zero modes:
$$
\mathcal{D} (O_{CDW}) = O_{SC}, \quad \mathcal{D} (O_{SC}) = O_{CDW} \tag{20.9}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Define $\mathcal{D}$ as an `AlgEquiv` between two different Luttinger Liquid instances parameterized by $g$ and $1/g$.
- Auxiliary Lemma: Show that the charge sectors $N_R, N_L$ map appropriately to realize the $\varphi \leftrightarrow \theta$ exchange algebraically.
- Apply $\mathcal{D}$ step-by-step to the explicit terms of the Hamiltonian and the vertex operators to prove the dualities.
