### Chapter 20: CDW Correlators and the Topological Duality Theorem

We evaluate macroscopic order parameters like **Charge Density Wave (CDW)** and anomalous pairing algebraically. The formalization strictly follows [Appendix A05](../appendices/a05_exponentials_klein_and_vertex_scope.md), [Appendix A08](../appendices/a08_states_and_correlations.md), and [Appendix A09](../appendices/a09_duality_spin_and_umklapp.md).

#### 20.1 Macroscopic Order Parameters on the Lattice

**Definition 20.1 (CDW and Pairing Order Parameters).**
The local macroscopic order parameters on the discrete lattice $\Lambda$ are defined strictly using fundamental fermions:
$$
O_{CDW}(x) := c^\dagger_{(R, x)} c_{(L, x)} \tag{20.1}
$$
$$
O_{SC}(x) := c_{(R, x)} c_{(L, x)} \tag{20.2}
$$

*Lean 4 Proof Strategy:*
- Define structures for `CDWOrderParameter` and `SCOrderParameter` in the discrete lattice algebra.
- Both must be expressed as elements of the fermion algebra generated by $c_{R, x}, c_{L, x}$, keeping track of lattice indices.
- Ensure the grading/parity of these operators is even, meaning they commute with the total fermion number parity.

**Lemma 20.2 (Bosonized Order Parameters on Budget Subspaces).**
Applying the projected Mattis-Mandelstam dictionary (Theorem 14.5), we map the finite CAR order parameters between specified sector budgets $P_{\text{target}} O(x) P_{\text{source}}$.
1. For $O_{CDW}(x) = c_{R,x}^\dagger c_{L,x}$, $c_{L,x}$ acts on $|N_L\rangle$, contributing source phase $\zeta^{N_L x}$ and lowering to $N_L-1$. Then $c_{R,x}^\dagger$ acts on $|N_R\rangle$, contributing adjoint source phase $\zeta^{-(N_R+1)x}$ and raising to $N_R+1$. Combining these yields the exact phase character $\zeta^{(N_L - N_R - 1)x}$:
$$
P_{\text{target}} O_{CDW}(x) P_{\text{source}} = \frac{1}{L} F_R^\dagger F_L \zeta^{(N_L - N_R - 1)x} \operatorname{expNil}\left[ W^-_R(x) - W^-_L(x) \right] \operatorname{expNil}\left[ W^+_R(x) - W^+_L(x) \right] \tag{20.3}
$$
2. For $O_{SC}(x) = c_{R,x} c_{L,x}$, both factors annihilate fermions, contributing source phases $\zeta^{N_L x}$ and $\zeta^{N_R x}$, yielding the exact phase character $\zeta^{(N_R + N_L)x}$:
$$
P_{\text{target}} O_{SC}(x) P_{\text{source}} = \frac{1}{L} F_R F_L \zeta^{(N_R + N_L)x} \operatorname{expNil}\left[ W^-_R(x) + W^-_L(x) \right] \operatorname{expNil}\left[ W^+_R(x) + W^+_L(x) \right] \tag{20.4}
$$

> [!NOTE]
> **Ground-State Test at $L=4$ ($\zeta=i$):**
> Evaluated on ground source and target sectors of cutoff zero ($K_{\text{in}} = K_{\text{out}} = 0$, where both $\operatorname{expNil}$ factors are identities) at $x=1$:
>
> | Operator at $x=1$ | Source $(N_R, N_L)$ | Target $(N_R', N_L')$ | Exact CAR Coefficient | Formula (20.3)–(20.4) |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | $O_{CDW}$ | $(0, 0)$ | $(1, -1)$ | $i/4$ | $\frac{1}{4} (-1) \cdot (-1) \cdot \zeta^{-1} = i/4$ |
> | $O_{SC}$ | $(1, 0)$ | $(0, -1)$ | $i/4$ | $\frac{1}{4} \cdot 1 \cdot \zeta^{1} = i/4$ |
>
> The corrected phase characters $\zeta^{(N_L - N_R - 1)x}$ and $\zeta^{(N_R + N_L)x}$ match the exact finite CAR matrix elements identically.

#### 20.2 Abstract Vertex Operators and Quasi-Free Correlators

**Definition 20.3 (Finite Logarithmic Kernel).**
We define the finite algebraic logarithmic kernel:
$$
D_1(x, y) := \sum_{m=1}^{M} \frac{1}{m} \left( 1 - \cos\left( \frac{2\pi m (x-y)}{L} \right) \right) \tag{20.5}
$$

**Definition 20.4 (Abstract Weyl / Vertex Order Parameters).**
In the abstract CCR/Weyl algebra $\mathfrak{A}_{\mathrm{CCR}}$, we define the abstract vertex order parameters:
$$
\mathcal{V}_{CDW}(x) := \frac{1}{L} F_R^\dagger F_L \zeta^{(N_L - N_R - 1)x} :\! \exp\left[ W^-_R(x) - W^-_L(x) + W^+_R(x) - W^+_L(x) \right] \!: \tag{20.6}
$$
$$
\mathcal{V}_{SC}(x) := \frac{1}{L} F_R F_L \zeta^{(N_R + N_L)x} :\! \exp\left[ W^-_R(x) + W^-_L(x) + W^+_R(x) + W^+_L(x) \right] \!: \tag{20.7}
$$
These abstract vertex operators live in the completion of the CCR/Weyl algebra, distinct from the finite-dimensional CAR operators $O_{CDW}(x), O_{SC}(x)$.

**Theorem 20.5 (Correlators in the Abstract Quasi-Free CCR State).**
Applying the exact algebraic BCH formula (Lemma 8.5) and evaluating over the abstract quasi-free CCR functional $\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}}$ (Definition 19.1), the abstract vertex correlators evaluate to exact equalities:
$$
\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}} \left( \mathcal{V}_{CDW}^\dagger(x) \mathcal{V}_{CDW}(y) \right) = \frac{C_0}{L^2} \zeta^{-(N_L - N_R - 1)(x-y)} \exp \left[ -2g \cdot D_1(x, y) \right] \tag{20.8}
$$
$$
\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}} \left( \mathcal{V}_{SC}^\dagger(x) \mathcal{V}_{SC}(y) \right) = \frac{C_0'}{L^2} \zeta^{-(N_R + N_L)(x-y)} \exp \left[ -\frac{2}{g} \cdot D_1(x, y) \right] \tag{20.9}
$$
where $g = (c-s)^2 > 0$ is the Luttinger parameter from `LuttingerParams` (Definition 18.5), and $C_0, C_0'$ are determined by zero-mode/Klein expectations.

> [!WARNING]
> The scalar Gaussian exponential $\exp[-2g D_1(x,y)]$ is an evaluation of the abstract quasi-free CCR functional $\omega_{\tilde{\Omega}}$, **not** an exact finite CAR Fock expectation. On a finite CAR lattice with $L=4, h=2$, direct computation on the bare vacuum gives $\langle\Omega|O_{CDW}^\dagger(0) O_{CDW}(0)|\Omega\rangle = 1/4$ and $\langle\Omega|O_{CDW}^\dagger(1) O_{CDW}(0)|\Omega\rangle = -1/8$, whereas the scalar exponential formula gives positive value $\frac{1}{4} e^{-2}$. The abstract vertex model and the finite CAR observable must be kept strictly distinct.

#### 20.3 Topological Duality Isomorphism

**Definition 20.6 (Parameterized Model and Duality Map).**
A Luttinger Liquid model instance $\mathcal{M}(P)$ is specified by its parameter record $P = (u, c, s, g, g^{-1}) \in \mathtt{LuttingerParams}$, cutoffs $(L, M, K)$, and Hamiltonian $H_{\text{Lutt}}(P)$.
The Duality map $\mathcal{D}: \mathcal{M}(P) \to \mathcal{M}(P^*)$ associates to $P$ the dual record $P^* = (u, c, -s, g^{-1}, g)$, satisfying:
$$
g(P^*) = \frac{1}{g(P)}.
$$
At the operator level, $\mathcal{D}$ exchanges the dual fields $\phi \leftrightarrow \theta$ (i.e. $\varphi_R \leftrightarrow \varphi_R, \varphi_L \leftrightarrow -\varphi_L$), maps charge sectors $(N_R, N_L) \to (N_R, -N_L)$, and exchanges the abstract vertex operators:
$$
\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{V}_{CDW}) = \mathcal{V}_{SC}, \qquad \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{V}_{SC}) = \mathcal{V}_{CDW} \tag{20.10}
$$

**Theorem 20.7 (The Exact Duality Equivalence).**
Under the duality map $\mathcal{D}$, the Hamiltonian transforms as:
$$
\mathcal{D} \left( H_{\text{Lutt}}(P) \right) = H_{\text{Lutt}}(P^*) \tag{20.11}
$$
preserving all spectra, phases, and commutation relations between dual models.
