### Chapter 6: The Lattice AQFT Net

In algebraic quantum field theory (AQFT), the primary objects of study are not isolated states, but the local algebras of observables associated with spatial regions. On the finite spatial lattice $\Lambda = \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}$, this structure is formalized as an exact net of finite-dimensional unital $C^*$-subalgebras of $\mathrm{Operators}(L) = \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathcal{F}(L))$, as implemented and proven in `Bosonize.Core.Ch06LocalNet`.

**No Axiomatic Assumptions:**
We assume no abstract physical AQFT axioms. Because every local algebra $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ is constructed constructively from the position CAR operators $c_x, c_x^\dagger$ acting on the finite-dimensional Fock space $\mathcal{F}(L)$, all standard AQFT properties (isotony, additivity, twisted locality, and global irreducibility) are rigorous mathematical theorems.

---

#### 6.1 Definitions and Structural Architecture

**Definition 6.1 (Local Algebras via Generators).**
Let $I \subseteq \Lambda$ be any spatial region (`Region L := Set (Ch01.Lattice L)`).

1. **Local Generators (`localGenerators`):**
   $$
   \mathcal{G}(I) := \{ c_x^{\phantom{\dagger}} \mid x \in I \} \cup \{ c_x^\dagger \mid x \in I \} \tag{6.1a}
   $$

2. **Local Subalgebra (`localAlgebra`):**
   $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ is defined as the unital complex subalgebra generated by $\mathcal{G}(I)$:
   $$
   \mathfrak{A}(I) := \mathrm{AlgGen}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathcal{G}(I)) = \mathrm{Algebra.adjoin} \ \mathbb{C} \ \mathcal{G}(I) \tag{6.1}
   $$
   Because $\mathcal{G}(I)$ is closed under the Hilbert adjoint ($(c_x)^\dagger = c_x^\dagger$ and $(c_x^\dagger)^\dagger = c_x$), $\mathfrak{A}(I)$ is automatically a star-subalgebra without requiring an axiomatic star completion.

3. **Empty Region:** Over the empty set, the algebra collapses to the scalar multiples of the identity: $\mathfrak{A}(\emptyset) = \mathbb{C} \cdot I$ (`local_algebra_empty`).

---

**Definition 6.2 (Global Parity Map & $\mathbb{Z}_2$-Grading).**
Let $\Gamma$ be the global parity operator on $\mathcal{F}(L)$ (`Ch04.parity`), satisfying $\Gamma^2 = I$ and $\Gamma^\dagger = \Gamma$.

1. **Parity Conjugation Map (`parityMap`):**
   The linear endomorphism on the operator space is:
   $$
   \alpha : \mathrm{Operators}(L) \to \mathrm{Operators}(L), \qquad \alpha(A) := \Gamma A \Gamma \tag{6.2}
   $$
   satisfying $\alpha \circ \alpha = \mathrm{id}$ and $\alpha(A B) = \alpha(A) \alpha(B)$.

2. **Local Homogeneous Subspaces (`localPart`):**
   For each parity degree $\sigma \in \mathbb{Z}_2 = \{0, 1\}$ (`Degree := Fin 2`), the $\sigma$-homogeneous local subspace is defined by intersecting the local algebra with the kernel eigenspace:
   $$
   \mathfrak{A}_\sigma(I) := \mathfrak{A}(I) \cap \ker(\alpha - (-1)^\sigma \cdot \mathrm{id}) \tag{6.3}
   $$
   - **Even Part $\mathfrak{A}_+(I) \equiv \mathfrak{A}_0(I)$ (`evenPart`):** Observables satisfying $\alpha(A) = A$. This forms a unital subalgebra.
   - **Odd Part $\mathfrak{A}_-(I) \equiv \mathfrak{A}_1(I)$ (`oddPart`):** Observables satisfying $\alpha(A) = -A$. This forms a linear subspace (not closed under multiplication, since $\mathrm{odd} \times \mathrm{odd} = \mathrm{even}$).

3. **Parity Projections (`evenProjection`, `oddProjection`):**
   $$
   P_+ := \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{id} + \alpha), \qquad P_- := \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{id} - \alpha) \tag{6.3a}
   $$
   providing the exact direct-sum grading $\mathfrak{A}(I) = \mathfrak{A}_+(I) \oplus \mathfrak{A}_-(I)$.

---

**Definition 6.2b (Words and Homogeneous Word Spans).**
Because algebraic adjoin induction does not preserve parity on partial linear sums, Lean builds homogeneous elements via words of spatial operators:

- A letter $a = (x, b) \in \Lambda \times \mathrm{Bool}$ represents $c_x^\dagger$ (if $b = \mathrm{true}$) or $c_x$ (if $b = \mathrm{false}$).

- A word $w = [a_1, \dots, a_m]$ evaluates to the product $O(w) = O(a_1) \cdots O(a_m)$ (`wordOperator`).

- The parity of a word is simply its length mod 2:
  $$
  \alpha(O(w)) = (-1)^{|w|} O(w)
  $$

- **Word Span Identification (`local_part_eq_word_span`):**
  Lean proves that the algebraic eigenspace $\mathfrak{A}_\sigma(I)$ coincides identically with the linear span of supported words of length $|w| \equiv \sigma \pmod 2$.

#### 6.2 Theorems & Verification of the AQFT Net Axioms

**Theorem 6.3 (Isotony & Additivity).**
For all spatial regions $I, J \subseteq \Lambda$:

1. **Isotony (`local_algebra_mono`):**
   $$
   I \subseteq J \implies \mathfrak{A}(I) \subseteq \mathfrak{A}(J)
   $$

2. **Additivity (`local_algebra_union`):**
   $$
   \mathfrak{A}(I \cup J) = \mathfrak{A}(I) \vee \mathfrak{A}(J)
   $$
   where $\vee$ is the supremum in the lattice of subalgebras (`⊔`).
*Proof:* Proved directly via Mathlib's `Algebra.adjoin_mono` and `Algebra.adjoin_union`.

---

**Theorem 6.4 (Twisted Locality / Graded Commutation).**
Let $I, J \subseteq \Lambda$ be disjoint spatial regions ($I \cap J = \emptyset$). For any homogeneous elements $A \in \mathfrak{A}_\sigma(I)$ and $B \in \mathfrak{A}_\tau(J)$ with parities $\sigma, \tau \in \{0, 1\}$:

$$
A B = (-1)^{\sigma \cdot \tau} B A \tag{6.4}
$$

*Proof Strategy in Lean 4 (`twisted_locality`):*

1. **Base Letter Anticommutation:** For any $x \in I$ and $y \in J$, because $I \cap J = \emptyset$, $x \ne y$. By the position CAR (Theorem 5.3), all four letter combinations anticommute:
   $$
   \{c_x^{\phantom{\dagger}}, c_y^{\phantom{\dagger}}\} = 0, \quad \{c_x^{\phantom{\dagger}}, c_y^\dagger\} = 0, \quad \{c_x^\dagger, c_y^{\phantom{\dagger}}\} = 0, \quad \{c_x^\dagger, c_y^\dagger\} = 0
   $$

2. **Word Permutation (`disjoint_word_swap`):**
   Swapping two words $w_1$ (supported in $I$) and $w_2$ (supported in $J$) requires $|w_1| \cdot |w_2|$ letter swaps, each contributing a factor of $(-1)$:
   $$
   O(w_1) O(w_2) = (-1)^{|w_1| \cdot |w_2|} O(w_2) O(w_1) = (-1)^{\sigma \cdot \tau} O(w_2) O(w_1)
   $$

3. **Linear Extension:** Since $\mathfrak{A}_\sigma(I)$ is spanned by homogeneous words (`local_part_eq_word_span`), bilinearity extends the relation to all homogeneous operators.

**Corollary 6.4b (Strict Even Locality):**
If $A \in \mathfrak{A}_+(I)$ and $B \in \mathfrak{A}_+(J)$ are even observables, $\sigma = \tau = 0$, so $(-1)^{0 \cdot 0} = 1$:
$$
[A, B] = A B - B A = 0 \tag{6.4a}
$$
The even subnet $\{\mathfrak{A}_+(I)\}_{I \subseteq \Lambda}$ is strictly local in the bosonic sense!

---

**Theorem 6.5 (Global Algebra Irreducibility).**
Evaluated over the full spatial lattice $\Lambda$, the local algebra generates the entire non-commutative operator algebra:
$$
\mathfrak{A}(\Lambda) = \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathcal{F}(L)) \tag{6.5}
$$

*Constructive Proof via Ordered Matrix Units (`global_algebra_eq_top`):*
Rather than relying on non-constructive dimension counting, Lean explicitly constructs every matrix unit $|S\rangle\langle T|$ inside $\mathfrak{A}(\Lambda)$:

1. **Recovering Momentum Operators:** By inverse Fourier transform (Theorem 5.4), each momentum generator $c_k, c_k^\dagger$ belongs to $\mathfrak{A}(\Lambda)$ (`momentum_annihilation_mem_global`, `momentum_creation_mem_global`).

2. **Vacuum Projector (`vacuumProjector`):**
   The orthogonal projector onto the empty Fock state $|\emptyset\rangle$ is constructed as the product:
   $$
   P_\emptyset := \prod_{k \in \Lambda^*} (I - c_k^\dagger c_k^{\phantom{\dagger}}) = |\emptyset\rangle\langle\emptyset| \in \mathfrak{A}(\Lambda)
   $$

3. **Ordered Creation & Annihilation Strings:**
   For any subset configuration $S \subseteq \Lambda^*$, let:
   $$
   C(S) := \prod_{k \in S}^{\downarrow} c_k^\dagger, \qquad A(S) := \prod_{k \in S}^{\uparrow} c_k^{\phantom{\dagger}}
   $$
   with sign factor $\sigma(S) = (-1)^{|S|(|S|-1)/2}$. Then:
   $$
   |S\rangle = \sigma(S) C(S) |\emptyset\rangle
   $$

4. **Constructing Elementary Matrix Units (`ordered_matrix_unit_eq_matrix_unit`):**
   For any configurations $S, T \subseteq \Lambda^*$, the rank-one operator $|S\rangle\langle T|$ is given by:
   $$
   |S\rangle\langle T| = \sigma(S) \sigma(T) \cdot C(S) P_\emptyset A(T) \in \mathfrak{A}(\Lambda)
   $$

5. **Conclusion:** Since the elementary matrix units $\{|S\rangle\langle T|\}_{S, T \subseteq \Lambda^*}$ span $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{C}}(\mathcal{F}(L))$, $\mathfrak{A}(\Lambda) = \top$. This establishes that the finite CAR representation on $\mathcal{F}(L)$ is completely irreducible.
