\section{Introduction and Canonical Anticommutation Relations}
The Canonical Anticommutation Relations (CAR) govern the algebra of fermionic creation and annihilation operators. For a finite-dimensional one-particle carrier space $V \simeq \mathbb{C}^d$ with an orthonormal basis indexed by a finite set $I$ of cardinality $d$, the abstract CAR $C^*$-algebra $\mathrm{CAR}(V)$ is the unital $*$-algebra generated by elements $\{c_i \mid i \in I\}$ satisfying:
\begin{equation}
\{c_i, c_j^\dagger\} = \delta_{i, j} I, \qquad \{c_i, c_j\} = 0, \qquad \{c_i^\dagger, c_j^\dagger\} = 0.
\end{equation}

\section{Constructive Fock Space and Fermionic Signs}
The standard Fock representation is realized on the exterior algebra $\mathcal{F} = \bigwedge V$. The state space has exact finite dimension:
\begin{equation}
\dim \mathcal{F} = 2^d.
\end{equation}
A basis for $\mathcal{F}$ is given by occupation configurations, represented by subsets $\sigma \subset I$. The fermionic creation operator $c_i^\dagger$ acts on the basis state $|\sigma\rangle$ by:
\begin{equation}
c_i^\dagger |\sigma\rangle = \begin{cases}
\sigma(i, \sigma)\, |\sigma \cup \{i\}\rangle, & \text{if } i \notin \sigma, \\
0, & \text{if } i \in \sigma,
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $\sigma(i, \sigma) = (-1)^{\#\{j \in \sigma \mid j < i\}}$ is the fermionic sign factor determined by a chosen total ordering on $I$.

\section{Key Results and Formal Discoveries}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Atomic Sign Lemma:} The verification that $\{c_i, c_j^\dagger\} = \delta_{i, j} I$ reduces to an atomic sign identity involving the parity of counting sets between $i$ and $j$.
    \item \textbf{Jordan--Wigner Correspondence:} On a linearly ordered finite set, the CAR algebra is isomorphic to a tensor product of Clifford / Pauli matrix algebras.
    \item \textbf{Adjoint Compatibility:} The algebraic adjoint $c_i^\dagger$ matches the genuine Hilbert space adjoint $(c_i)^\dagger$ under the canonical inner product.
\end{itemize}
