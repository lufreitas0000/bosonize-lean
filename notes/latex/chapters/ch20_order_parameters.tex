\chapter[Finite Order Parameters and Formal Vertices]{Finite Order Parameters, CDW, Superconductivity, and Formal Vertices}
\label{ch:order-parameters}

The preceding chapters established two parallel mathematical languages: the exact
finite-dimensional fermionic CAR system on a discrete lattice, and the uncompressed
algebraic CCR representation of density modes with its positive Bogoliubov vacuum functional.
We now apply this architecture to the central physical phenomena of one-dimensional
many-body systems: charge-density-wave (CDW) ordering, superconducting (SC) pairing,
and the construction of fermion vertex operators.

A fundamental question arises when defining composite order parameters:
\emph{what happens when two fields are multiplied in a restricted low-energy subspace?}
We demonstrate that intermediate energy-budget projections introduce an explicit,
non-vanishing residual measuring discarded virtual excursions.  Furthermore, we resolve
the status of Klein factors by establishing the Finite-Charge Unitary Lowering No-Go Theorem:
on a finite Hilbert space, Klein factors cannot act as unitary charge-lowering operators,
but must be realized via constructive Jordan--Wigner basis-occupation cocycles.
Finally, we analyze formal vertex operators, show how zero modes cancel residual oscillator
phases to restore separated-point anticommutation in the continuum limit, and construct an
exact algebraic duality mapping between the CDW and superconducting sectors.
